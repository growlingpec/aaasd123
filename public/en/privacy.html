Privacy policy

Applies to Premier (eide.pro). Updated: October 12, 2026.

Data we process

  • When registering: your email address, password (stored only as a hash) and name, or information from your Google account (name and email) if you sign in with Google.
  • When using the service: your chat history, projects and generated websites linked to your account.
  • Technical data: IP address and basic request logs (to prevent abuse).

Your language choice (RU/EN) is saved in a functional cookie and browser local storage. When switching languages, your message draft is temporarily saved in tab storage.

How we use your data

  • To sign in to your account and operate the service.
  • Your messages are sent to the AI model you select (a provider connected by the administrator) to generate responses. Providers process them under their own terms.
  • To manage subscriptions and plan limits.

Storage and security

  • Data is stored on the operator’s server; regular backups are made.
  • Passwords are stored as hashes; keys and secrets are kept in server settings inaccessible to users.
  • To delete your account, contact the administrator and request deletion of your data.

Payments

Subscription payments are processed by a third-party payment provider. The service does not request or store payment card details.

Contact

For personal-data requests, use the website contact/feedback section or email the administrator.
